Featured Project

SunTrack

Field Sales & Lead Management

SunTrack is a field-sales lead management platform designed to help teams manage prospects, mobilisers, follow-ups and field activity more effectively.

Problem

Field-sales teams can lose potential customers when leads, referrals and follow-ups are tracked informally.

Solution

SunTrack provides a structured workflow for capturing leads, assigning responsibility, recording follow-ups and monitoring progress until a prospect is ready for the real-world sale process.
